How getting support works

No forms to fill in for weeks, no waiting lists to join. Just a friendly conversation and a plan made around you.

  1. Reach out

    Request a call, send a WhatsApp message, or walk into a clinic. Use a nickname if you like.

  2. Have a chat

    A trained intake volunteer listens, answers first questions and asks what matters most to you.

  3. Get your plan

    Together you choose the programs that fit, whether that's one clinic visit or several months of support.

  4. Keep going

    Your contact checks in regularly. When you're ready, you can become a mentor for someone else.

About us

A soft place to land and a strong place to grow

The Divorced Women Initiative exists so that no woman has to face divorce without information, support or friendship.

Why we started

Too many women leave a marriage without knowing their rights, without savings in their own name, and carrying the weight of what others will say. Many are told to stay quiet and endure.

We believe divorce can be the beginning of a life a woman chooses for herself. Our work is to make that beginning safer, clearer and far less lonely, with practical help and people who stay beside her for as long as she needs.

Today we bring together volunteer lawyers, counsellors, financial coaches and women who have rebuilt their own lives, all working toward the same thing: helping you find your way.

Our mission

To walk beside women through every stage of divorce with free legal, financial, emotional and practical support, so they can rebuild with confidence.

Our vision

A world where every woman leaving a marriage is met with understanding instead of judgement, and has everything she needs to thrive.

The people behind the petals

What guides us

No judgement

Every woman's story is her own. We never ask you to justify your decision, only how we can help.

Confidential by default

What you share stays with us, always.

Practical first

Feelings matter, and so do rent, school fees and court dates. We help with both.

Women helping women

What to expect at your first visit

Feeling nervous is completely normal. Here's exactly what happens when you reach out.

  • A warm welcome from a trained volunteer, with no paperwork needed to start.
  • A private conversation about what's happening and what you need most.
  • Clear answers to your most urgent questions, or a booking with someone who can answer them.
  • A simple plan you agree on together, with one named contact person.
  • A follow-up within one week, by whichever method feels safe for you.
